# Product

## Register

- Personal site for Kan Yilmaz.
- Keep the existing restrained, editorial visual language.

## Users

- Kan, friends, and family comparing shared anime ratings.
- Public visitors browsing Kan's interests and recommendations.

## Product Purpose

- Present personal writing, projects, interests, and lists clearly.
- Make the anime tier list a fast visual summary of the family's shared ratings.

## Brand Personality

- Direct, opinionated, curious, and lightly playful.
- Concise writing with useful detail revealed on demand.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards.
- Overdecorated anime fan sites.
- Dense tables that hide the covers and titles.
- Effects or motion that compete with the content.

## Design Principles

1. Put the content first.
2. Make ranking logic legible at a glance.
3. Reveal voter detail and comments on demand.
4. Preserve the typography and navigation of the main site.
5. Use color as reinforcement, never as the only label.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast.
- Support keyboard focus wherever hover reveals information.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep readable labels on every cover and tier.

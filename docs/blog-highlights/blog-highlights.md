# Blog highlights

`_data/post_highlights.yml` maps Jekyll post slugs to milestone labels,
accessible descriptions, and platforms. The blog index renders each badge
in the top-left corner of its card image, inside the existing article link.
HN badges use the Hacker News logo from the site's existing Font Awesome set.

Kan supplied the `HN #1` and `+200k views` milestones on September 30, 2026.
These are editorial milestones, not live counters or independently verified
peak-rank or view-count measurements. Updating a badge does not change the
article's publication date or RSS modification date.
